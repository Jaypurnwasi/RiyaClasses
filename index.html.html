Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee structure at BrightMind Academy?
▼
Our fees are very reasonable and competitive. They vary by class (1–5, 6–8, 9–10, 11–12) and subject combination. Contact us at +91-9876543210 for the exact fee chart. We also offer instalment payment options for your convenience.
What are the batch timings?
▼
We offer multiple batches — Morning (7 AM – 10 AM), Afternoon (1 PM – 4 PM), and Evening (5 PM – 8 PM) — so you can choose what works best for your school schedule.
Do you offer free demo classes?
▼
Yes! Every new student gets a free demo class before enrolment. This helps you and your child experience our teaching style, environment, and faculty before making any commitment.
Which boards do you cover — CBSE or MP Board?
▼
We cover both CBSE and MP Board curricula for Class 1 to Class 12. Our faculty is trained in both syllabi and we use board-specific study materials and exam strategies.
What subjects are available for coaching?
▼
We offer coaching in Mathematics, Science, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Social Science, Computer Science, and Commerce subjects (Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics). All subjects are available for both CBSE and MP Board.
Where is the institute located?
▼
BrightMind Academy is located at 123, Near City Center Mall, Indore, Madhya Pradesh – 462001. We are easily accessible by public transport and have parking available nearby.
How small are the batches?
▼
We maintain a strict cap of 12–15 students per batch to ensure personal attention for every student. We believe small batch sizes are the key to genuine academic improvement.
How do you keep parents updated about their child's progress?
▼
We share monthly progress reports, weekly test scores, and conduct Parent-Teacher Meetings every quarter. You can also reach your child's teacher directly on WhatsApp for any concern.
